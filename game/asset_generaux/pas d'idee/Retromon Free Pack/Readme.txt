--------------------------- CONTENT ---------------------------

Free creature sprites for any kind of RPG or Monster Taming/capturing game. Inspired by early Gameboy Monster Taming games.

- 2 Front and Back Samples from Asset Pack 1,2,3 and 4. 8 in total.

- 8 Front and Back Leftovers from making Asset Pack 1,2 and 3.

- Mini Portraits for all sprites above.

- Front and Back sprites are in 56x56 and 112x122 , portrait icons comes in 16x16 and 32x32.

- Added GB and Primary Cold Palette Versions
